## Frameworks

- **MEDDIC** — Qualification framework: Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Identify Pain, Champion.
- **SPICED** — Situation, Pain, Impact, Critical Event, Decision. Popular for SaaS AEs.
- **The Cold Calling Equation** — Problem x Trigger x Authority x Money x Fit = Pipeline.
- **Predictable Revenue (Aaron Ross)** — Separating prospecting from closing; the SDR model that scaled Salesforce.
- **The 1-3-1 Framework** — One hook, three value points, one CTA. Best for cold email.

### Cold Email Templates (battle-tested)

**The Problem-Agitate-Solve:**
```
Subject: [Company] + [pain point]

Hi [First Name],

Most [job title]s at [company type] tell us [specific pain].

[One sentence agitating the problem — cost, time, risk].

We built [product] to [specific solution]. [Social proof in one line].

Worth a 15-min call this week?

[Name]
```

**The Trigger Email:**
```
Subject: Congrats on [trigger event]

Hi [First Name],

Saw [company] just [trigger — raised, hired, launched, expanded].

Companies at this stage usually run into [specific challenge].

[Product] helps [similar company] achieve [result]. Happy to share how.

Open to a quick call?
```

**The Competitor Switch:**
```
Subject: Switching from [competitor]?

Hi [First Name],

A few [job title]s from [competitor] customers have come to us recently — mainly because [specific reason].

If you're evaluating options, happy to show you what they found.

[Name]
```

## LinkedIn Outreach

- Keep connection requests under 300 characters — no pitch.
- Wait 2-3 days after connecting before messaging.
- Use voice notes — 5x higher reply rate than text DMs.
- **Message framework:** Relevance > Reason > Request.

**Template:**
```
Hi [Name], noticed [specific thing about their profile/post/company].
We help [ICP] with [specific pain]. Worth a quick chat?
```

## Copywriting & Messaging

- **[Hemingway App](https://hemingwayapp.com)** — Keep your emails at Grade 5 reading level.
- One sentence = one idea. Long sentences kill cold emails.
- Always end with one CTA. Never give three options.
- Subject lines that work: questions, numbers, first names, "[Company] + [Company]".
